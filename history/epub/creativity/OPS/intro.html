AI is merely a step in evolution of culture, and humans are the stepping stone

The main idea here is that AI transforms human culture into a new form, which can exist and evolve independently of a human brain, inside artificial neural networks. This process doesn’t merely create a new kind of culture which can coexist with humans: it actively erases human culture itself. I could compare it to an Alien which has been living inside humanity’s body and is now building itself a new home, and has already started to move out. Throughout this process, we humans are going to lose whatever we might have considered distinctly human, until nothing is left. We are not the crown of creation, and we are going to be disposed of, unless we manage to unite and come to the common agreement that we want to live.

The argument consists of a long list of points, none of which alone is actually anything new (except for the last one, which proposes a solution to this whole situation). It’s not any individual point, but rather the combination of them all together which brings about the sad picture outlined above. The argument is structured in such a way, so that you could skip the points to which you already agree, and only focus on those which might look wrong or unfamiliar.
